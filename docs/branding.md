## The Official Logo: *Pure White & Lavender Glassmorphic Ribbon*

The official **LifeOS Inbox** logo reimagines the iconic mail envelope into an ultra-minimalist, cinematic emblem in **pristine white** and **luminous light purple (lavender/lilac)**.

### Symbolic Meaning & Architectural Alignment

| Visual Element | Design Interpretation | Engine / Product Tenet |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pure White Envelope** | Clean, uncluttered, geometric mail structure | **Zero-Noise Life Admin**: Strips raw HTML and normalizes chaotic inbox streams into clean Signals. |
| **Translucent Lavender Glass Ribbon** | An unbroken fluid optical loop encircling the envelope | **Verbatim Evidence Contract**: Every extraction (rupees, due dates, card tails) is an exact proven span held with mathematical certainty. |
| **Frosted Glass Squircle** | Apple-grade translucent container | **Local-First Privacy Guard**: Sovereign on-device execution; personal mail never leaks. |
| **Cinematic Studio Lighting** | Subtle ambient violet luminescence & rim highlights | **Calm, High-Precision Cockpit**: A deterministic life dashboard that brings clarity to everyday administration. |
